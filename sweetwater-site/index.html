MEET YOUR HOST

Grant Thomashoff

Founder |Sweetwater ExperiencesSoetwaters Atlantic

Cape Town born and ocean raised, Grant Thomashoff has spent more than four decades exploring the Peninsula’s coast, mountains and people. A lifelong surfer, provincial swimmer and water polo player, and qualified geochemist, he brings a distinctive depth of local knowledge to every journey.

His varied career has taken him from service in the South African Air Force to working as a lifeguard, swimming instructor and children’s camp counsellor in Manhattan, New York, as well as tour guiding and managing restaurants in Australia. After travelling across five continents, he returned to his Cape Town roots, where he successfully built and sold a cosmetics company that spanned Africa.

Today, Grant owns a coastal Airbnb and a restaurant, sharing his personal experiences and local knowledge with discerning guests seeking something extraordinary. Among the many distinguished guests he has served and hosted are King Charles III and Usain Bolt’s family.

Through Sweetwater Experiences Tours and Soetwaters Atlantic Fire Dining, Grant combines thoughtful personal service with a natural gift for storytelling, sharing the Cape he knows intimately — its characters, flavours, landscapes and untold stories.

Discover the Cape not merely as a visitor, but as a privileged guest of someone who has spent a lifetime calling it home.
